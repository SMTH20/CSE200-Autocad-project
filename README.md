# 🏠 Single Studio Apartment

### Computer Aided Engineering Drawing Project

A compact and functional studio apartment designed using **AutoCAD and 3D Visualization**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents the design of a **Single Studio Apartment** within a limited space of **28 ft × 22 ft**. The design focuses on efficient space utilization, functionality, comfortable movement, natural lighting, ventilation, and aesthetic appeal.

The project includes both a **2D Floor Plan** and **3D Visualization** of the proposed apartment.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Design a compact yet functional studio apartment
- Utilize the available space efficiently
- Include all essential living spaces
- Maintain proper circulation between different areas
- Provide natural lighting and ventilation
- Create both 2D and 3D representations
- Develop skills in architectural planning and visualization

---

## 🏠 Apartment Layout

| Area | Dimension |
|---|---|
| 🏠 Total Area | 28 × 22 ft |
| 🛋️ Living & Sleeping Area | 14 × 12 ft |
| 🍳 Kitchen | 8 × 6 ft |
| 🍽️ Dining Area | 6 × 6 ft |
| 🚿 Washroom | 5 × 4 ft |

---

## 📐 2D Floor Plan

The **2D Floor Plan** provides a detailed top-view representation of the apartment with proper dimensions, labels, doors, windows, and functional spaces.

### Key Features

- Main entrance opening into the living and sleeping area
- Kitchen positioned adjacent to the main area
- Dining area located near the kitchen
- Washroom placed in a corner for privacy
- Strategically placed windows for natural light and ventilation

---

## 🧱 3D Visualization

The project also includes a **3D Visualization** of the studio apartment to present a realistic view of the proposed design.

### 3D Model Includes

- 🛏️ Bed and sleeping arrangement
- 🛋️ Sofa and living space
- 🍽️ Dining table
- 🍳 Kitchen cabinets and counter
- 🚿 Washroom fixtures
- 🧱 10 ft wall height
- 🏠 Modern flat roof

The 3D visualization helps demonstrate the **spatial relationships, interior arrangement, lighting, and overall aesthetics** of the apartment.
